the shift from search engines to recommendation engines

sep 2026 · 3 min · rip ten blue links

Google used to give you homework: 10 links, good luck, do the reading yourself. you were the synthesizer. unpaid intern of the internet.

now AI does the reading, forms an opinion, and hands you one answer. confident. sometimes wrong. always confident, like me during viva.

that kills rank as we knew it. doesn't matter if you're result number 3 if the model never says your name. new game: be legible enough that the model trusts you.

mapping that trust layer at Beyond Search (about 50 queries in, watching phrasing flip winners).

if you run a product and the models ignore you: hi, that's literally what i'm building for.
